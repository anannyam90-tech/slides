Nails, Cobras & Broken Targets

The Nail Factory
Soviet quotas measured nails by total weight. So factories stopped making useful nails — and started making a handful of giant, useless ones.
The Cobra Bounty
Colonial India paid a bounty per dead cobra. So people started breeding cobras — just to kill them for cash.
